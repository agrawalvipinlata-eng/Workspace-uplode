Privacy Policy

SRBS International School — School Bus Tracking App ("Never Miss Bus")
Nauhjheel, Mathura (U.P.), India · Last updated: September 2026

This app is a private school utility made ONLY for the students, parents, teachers and staff of SRBS International School. Accounts are created only by the school — there is no public sign-up.

1. Data we collect

  • Student name, class, section and roll number (provided by the school)
  • Parent contact number and address (school records)
  • Optional profile photo (only if the user adds one)
  • School bus GPS location — shared ONLY from the driver's device and ONLY during an active bus trip
  • Attendance and fee records maintained by the school

2. What we DO NOT do

  • We never sell, rent or share personal data with third parties
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics trackers
  • Student devices are NEVER location-tracked — only the school bus is tracked
  • All data stays inside the school's own Google Firebase project

3. Who can access data

  • Student/Parent: their own profile, attendance, fees, and assigned bus
  • Class teacher: details and attendance of their own class only
  • School admin: all records, for school management
  • Students cannot see other students' personal details

4. Security

  • Password-protected login; only one device per account at a time
  • Server-side Firebase Security Rules enforce role-based access
  • The school can instantly deactivate any account

5. Children's privacy

The app is used by school students under teacher and parent supervision. Children cannot create accounts themselves. Parents may contact the school office at any time to review or delete their child's data.

6. Data retention & deletion

Data is kept only while the student/staff member is part of the school. To request deletion, contact the school office; the admin permanently deletes the account and all associated data.
